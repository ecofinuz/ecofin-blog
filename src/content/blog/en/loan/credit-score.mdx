## What Is a Credit Score?

In finance, accounting, and auditing, numbers need to "speak" to you. In other words, you need to draw useful analytical conclusions from financial statements. That is why analytics is used so often in finance. The role of financial analyst is also [one of the fastest-growing professions in the United States](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/financial-analysts.htm).

There are many analytical ratios in finance. For example, how much profit was made: return on investment and net profit margin. How well a company's daily operations are going: the cash conversion cycle. How responsibly a company treats society and nature: ESG rating. A credit score, or credit scoring, is one of those analytical indicators.

**A credit score** is a numerical indicator that shows how reliable a person taking a loan or borrowing money is. The name may sound complicated, but the idea is quite simple.

Imagine you have two friends. Let the first one be Toshmat. His work situation is unclear: one day he is in one _hustle_, the next day in another. He is always in debt, yet he has the newest phone in his hand and spends every month at Charvak. He loves videos with lines like "do not play with a man's luck." He always calls you "brother."

The second one is Eshmat. He is educated and works at a good place. He is settled, spends money carefully, goes to work, and if he has free time he drives a taxi. You have never heard anything shameful about him. Other than the mortgage he took for his home, he has no loans.

Now imagine both come to you on the same day and ask to borrow 3,000,000 soums. Naturally, you hesitate to lend money to Toshmat. Questions appear right away: "Will my money come back? When will it come back? Will I give it with my hands and then chase it with my feet?"

With Eshmat, no such questions come up. He has a job, a car, and a home. He does not waste money on unnecessary things. Your trust is high. His reputation in society is strong. You lend him money without extra questions because you trust that he will return it.

A credit score is the large-scale numerical expression of that trust. In the banking system, Toshmat's score would be low and Eshmat's would be high.

At the level of society, a bank does not personally know the people coming in for loans. It does not have the time or ability to ask their workplace or neighbors about them. The chance of getting objective information that way is also low.

That is why a bank takes factors such as a loan applicant's workplace, salary, credit history, whether they paid loans on time, assets, criminal history, and so on, then turns them into a single credit scoring system.

In Uzbekistan, this work is done by [KATM](https://infokredit.uz/) (the Credit Information Analytical Center) and the international firm [CRIF](https://www.crif.uz/). For several good reasons, credit scoring algorithms are not disclosed to the public.

## Credit Scoring And Loans

[As we said in the previous post](/en/loan/how-rates-are-determined/), a loan interest rate consists of four factors:

<div class="formula-block">
    <p class="formula-label">Formula</p>
    <p class="formula">(1 + R) = (1 + c) × (1 + r) × (1 + m) × (1 + π)</p>
    <p class="formula-note">R - nominal interest rate &nbsp;·&nbsp; c - base rate (the bank's cost of funds) &nbsp;·&nbsp; r - risk premium &nbsp;·&nbsp; m - bank profit margin &nbsp;·&nbsp; π - inflation</p>
</div>

The base rate, [inflation](/en/loan/inflation-and-loan/), the risk premium, and the profit margin. Credit scoring directly affects the risk premium.

The better your credit score, the lower the interest rate you should theoretically receive. This does not affect consumer loans that much. It is a more noticeable factor for governments, large companies, and mortgages.

Large corporations and governments around the world also receive credit ratings. This work is done by three giants: [S&P](https://uz.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%26P_Global_Ratings), [Moody's](https://uz.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moody%E2%80%99s_Ratings), and [Fitch](https://uz.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitch_Ratings). According to S&P's 2026 rating, [Uzbekistan is at BB/B](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/type/HTML/id/3568186). In other words, average.

This rating determines how much investment comes into Uzbekistan and under what conditions. The better our rating is, the more trust the financial world has in us.

In Uzbekistan, as an individual, if your credit score is poor, you may be offered credit at [very high rates](/en/loan/how-rates-are-determined/). Or you may not be approved at all. Because credit scoring is centralized by the state, banks can see your credit rating. Since the system is standardized by the state, banks trust your rating and decide whether to lend to you.

In other words, if you take a 49% loan from dear TBC Bank and then struggle and hide from payments, SQB Bank and Kapital Bank may also know about it if you come to them for another loan.

## How Does It Work?

Credit rating algorithms are kept strictly secret. The reason is that if the factors and their weight were public, _clever_ people would manipulate their scores. That would reduce banks' trust in credit scoring, raise the risk premium, and push rates higher for ordinary borrowers. That is why credit scoring algorithms are kept confidential.

Still, the general factors are known. The most important one is credit history. More important than salary, assets, and current debts.

Why? Because in Uzbekistan, especially in the regions, many people do not have official jobs or official income: market sellers, craftsmen, mechanics, taxi drivers, and small private entrepreneurs. In many Uzbek families, homes and cars are registered under the parents' names. Uzbeks also usually do not formalize personal debts with a notary. For these reasons, salary, assets, and current debts are not always accurate or objective factors.

For several reasons, Uzbekistan has strongly pushed the move from cash to electronic systems. For taxes, economic indicators, and control. From April 1, 2026, [gas stations no longer accept cash](https://zamin.uz/uz/ozbekiston/185468-rasman-ozbekistonda-1-apreldan-zapravkada-naqd-pul-otmaydi.html), for example. More on this in other posts.

---

Historically, credit scoring models placed heavy emphasis on credit history. Even if you have a lot of money, if you have no credit history, your credit score will be average. Today, however, other factors also receive more attention: turnover on plastic cards, employment history, age, gender, assets, and so on.

Credit scoring is updated and developed every year. As Uzbekistan's population moves from cash to plastic cards, the algorithms will become more accurate too.

## How Can You Get Your Score?

So, we have looked at the credit rating system in general. I will write a separate series about it one day, God willing. How does this system work in Uzbekistan, and more importantly, how can you find out your own score?

In Uzbekistan, credit scoring uses a 0-500 scale and is divided into 5 classes:

- **0-100 (E class)**
- **100-200 (D class)**
- **200-300 (C class)**
- **300-400 (B class)**
- **400-500 (A class)**

The higher your score, the better when you apply for credit. I have a job, official income, and taxes, but I have never taken a loan. And I do not plan to. My score came out average: 281. The reason is that I have no credit history.

To get your own credit score, follow these steps:

1. Go to the **my.gov.uz** portal
2. Search for the "Issuance of a credit history certificate" service. [Here is the link](https://my.gov.uz/en/service/411).
3. Authenticate with an electronic signature or One ID
4. Submit the request

The result is provided as an HTML file inside a .zip archive, for security and convenience for banks. It shows all active and closed loans, payment history, and your credit score.

## Low Score?

If your credit score is low and getting a loan is difficult, the best advice is simple: try to pay your loans on time.

But the best advice is this: in the long run, [try not to take loans at all](/en/loan/when-debt-works/). Your credit score may not be high, but you will live well without credit. You will sleep peacefully at night.

In practice, you cannot raise a credit score quickly. Algorithms analyze fixed characteristics, such as gender and demographics, and several years of financial data to create the score. This takes time. But there are several practical steps:

- **Pay existing loans on time.** This is the most influential factor. Even one month of delay hurts your score.
- **Close overdue payments as soon as possible.** If the debt remains, it is recorded as a new negative entry every month.
- **Avoid taking new loans.** Every credit application affects your score. Applying to several places in a short period sends a negative signal.
- **Be patient.** A good credit history is built over years.
- **Become wealthy.** Try to reach a point where you do not need credit. Financial freedom, especially for a man, is one of the greatest freedoms. Not being trapped by credit is a good thing.

## Conclusion

A credit score answers the first question a bank asks about you:

"Will this person repay the debt? Can we trust them?"

This process exists not only for individuals, but also for entire corporations and governments. The rules are just slightly different there. The essence is the same.

Remember the interest rate formula. The risk premium was a large component. A credit score is exactly what determines that risk premium - personally for you.
